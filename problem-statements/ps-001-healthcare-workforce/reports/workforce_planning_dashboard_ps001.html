Key Insight: Singapore's healthcare workforce shows strong, sustained growth across all five professions. Log-linear models (R2 ≥ 0.998) project supply surplus under baseline demand assumptions through 2030. However, nurses and allied health face the narrowest margins if aging-accelerated demand grows faster than projected.
  • Pharmacists (5.7% CAGR) and Allied Health (5.4% CAGR) are the fastest-growing professions.
  • Doctors (3.2% CAGR) have the slowest growth - policy focus required to maintain doctor-to-population ratios.
  • Hospital admission rates for 65+ are rising ~1%/year - a key demand risk factor to monitor.
